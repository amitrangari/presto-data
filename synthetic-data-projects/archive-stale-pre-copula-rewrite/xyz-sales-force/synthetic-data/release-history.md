# XYZ Company Sales Force Platform - Release History

## Project Overview
Enterprise Sales Platform with microservices architecture, micro frontends, and 50+ development teams delivering bi-weekly releases over 3 years (2022-2024).

**Architecture:**
- Micro Frontend Architecture
- 50+ Microservices (5-member teams each)
- Bi-weekly release cycle (26 releases/year)
- 3-year development timeline
